Common questions

We've thought about your concerns

✓

Will my child actually learn, or just copy answers?

Niel's Understanding Mode is designed to prevent passive copying. The AI guides students to reason through problems themselves, only revealing full answers when explicitly requested.

✓

Is it safe for my child to use?

Niel Academy is strictly an educational tool. The AI is tuned to stay focused on academic content and is designed to be appropriate and encouraging for secondary school students.

✓

My child has limited internet — will it still work?

Yes. Core exam materials and content can be downloaded in advance and accessed offline, so your child can study even when connectivity is unreliable.

✓

Is it aligned to what their school is actually teaching?

Absolutely. All content is built directly from official Cameroon GCE past papers and structured around the same syllabus your child's teachers follow.

Our Mission & Vision

Quality education shouldn't depend on where you're born.

Sub-Saharan Africa faces a severe teacher shortage, overcrowded classrooms, and a generation of students who deserve better. Niel Academy is building the solution — starting in Cameroon and growing across the continent.

The problem

A crisis that demands action now

15M

More teachers needed across Sub-Saharan Africa by 2030 to meet quality education goals (UNESCO)

85:1

Students per teacher in some Cameroonian classrooms — making individual support nearly impossible

58:1

Average pupil-to-trained-teacher ratio at primary level across the region (UNESCO)

The founder

Built from lived experience

OC

Othniel Che

Founder & Software Engineer

"As a secondary school student in Cameroon, I used platforms like Khan Academy to study after school. While helpful, these tools weren't built for our curriculum, exams or context. Years later, in 2025, I watched my younger sister face the same challenges. Very little had changed."

As a software engineer already working with AI, Othniel decided to build the platform he wished he had. Niel Academy is the result — a curriculum-aligned AI tutor designed specifically for Cameroonian and African students, built with pedagogy and affordability at its core.
