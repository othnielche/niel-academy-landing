# Niel Academy Website Improvement Directives

## Goal
Transform the website from a polished product landing page into a site that immediately communicates traction, credibility, differentiation, and long-term vision.

---

## 1. Hero Section
- Replace generic messaging with a value proposition focused on **personalized, curriculum-aligned AI learning**.
- Keep the CTA to download the app.
- Add a secondary CTA: **Watch a 60-second demo**.
- Display key traction metrics above the fold:
  - 1,000+ students reached
  - 503 active learners
  - Live since January 2026

---

## 2. Product Showcase
Replace mockups or generic illustrations with real screenshots demonstrating:
- AI tutoring
- Guided Mode
- Understanding Mode
- Automated grading
- Personalized recommendations
- Progress tracking

---

## 3. Explain the Competitive Advantage
Create a "Why Niel Academy?" section comparing:
- General AI tools
- Niel Academy

Highlight:
- Curriculum alignment
- Official mark schemes
- Adaptive tutoring
- Learning progress tracking
- Exam-focused workflow

---

## 4. Showcase the Grading Engine
Illustrate:
Student Answer
→ AI Evaluation
→ Mark Scheme
→ Feedback
→ Recommendations

Explain why this differs from a generic chatbot.

---

## 5. Add Social Proof
Include:
- Student count
- Active learners
- Launch date
- Self-funded status

Later add:
- Testimonials
- School partners
- Press mentions
- Awards

---

## 6. Founder Story
Add a concise founder section covering:
- Why Niel Academy was built
- The educational problem observed
- Long-term mission

Keep it authentic and brief.

---

## 7. Vision
Communicate that Niel Academy begins with Cameroon but is building curriculum-specific AI learning infrastructure for African education systems.

Avoid positioning the company as only a GCE application.

---

## 8. Evidence Instead of Generic Claims
Replace broad statements with specific ones.

Example:
Instead of:
"Personalized feedback"

Use:
"Receive detailed feedback based on official examination mark schemes."

---

## 9. FAQ
Include:
- Why not ChatGPT?
- Which examinations are supported?
- How accurate is grading?
- Can teachers use it?
- Pricing
- Offline capabilities
- Future curriculum expansion

---

## 10. Video
Create a short demonstration showing:
1. Student asks a question
2. AI tutoring
3. Student attempts answer
4. Automated grading
5. Personalized feedback
6. Recommended next questions

---

## 11. Press & Media
Create an About/Press page including:
- Company overview
- Founder bio
- High-resolution logo
- Screenshots
- Contact information
- Press kit

---

## 12. SEO
Naturally include keywords such as:
- AI tutor Cameroon
- Cameroon exam preparation
- Curriculum-aligned learning
- Personalized learning
- GCE past questions
- Probatoire
- Baccalauréat
- WAEC (future)

Avoid keyword stuffing.

---

## 13. Overall Messaging
Emphasize that Niel Academy is:
- Personalized
- Curriculum-aligned
- Assessment-driven
- Built for African education
- Designed to improve understanding rather than simply provide answers

The website should leave visitors believing they are seeing an education technology company with regional growth potential—not just another AI study app.
