About Niel Academy
Our Mission

We exist to help African students learn how to think — not just what to memorize.

Niel Academy is building a mobile-first learning platform that gives students preparing for national exams reliable access to structured learning materials and tools that promote real understanding. We start in Cameroon with GCE and TVEE exams, with a long-term vision to expand across Africa.

The Problem We’re Solving

Across many African education systems, students face three systemic barriers:

Fragmented access to learning materials
Past papers, notes, and explanations are scattered, inconsistent, or difficult to find.

Learning without understanding
Many students rely on memorization and copied solutions, which breaks down when exam questions change.

Global tools that don’t fit local realities
Existing AI and edtech platforms are built for foreign syllabuses and study styles, not local exam structures or constraints like limited connectivity.

These gaps create learning inequality, limit academic outcomes, and reduce students’ confidence in their ability to succeed.

Our Approach

We are building an exam-focused learning platform designed specifically for local curricula and constraints.

Our approach combines:

Free, open access to core learning materials
Past exam papers and notes are available to all students, regardless of ability to pay.

Understanding-first learning design
Students are encouraged to reason through problems before seeing solutions, building critical thinking rather than dependency.

Multiple learning modes
Learners can choose between guided support, independent reasoning, or full solutions depending on their needs and learning style.

Mobile-first, low-bandwidth design
The platform is built for the realities of African students, including offline access to core content.

Why This Matters

When students learn how to think through problems:

Academic performance improves

Confidence increases

Dropout risk decreases

Long-term learning outcomes compound beyond a single exam

We believe teaching students how to learn is one of the highest-leverage interventions for improving educational outcomes at scale.

Early Traction
